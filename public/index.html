Looking for a Specific Card?

Tell us what you are chasing and we will go and find it. We are at shows, breaks and auctions every week, so plenty of what we handle never gets listed.

You can be as specific or as loose as you like:

  • A particular card — set, year, parallel, numbering, graded or raw
  • A player — every Josh Allen rookie, or just his autos
  • A team — Patriots, Bills, Red Sox, Celtics, whatever you collect
  • A price point — tell us your budget and we will bring you options that fit

No obligation and no fee for looking. If we find it, we send you photos and a price; if you pass, that is fine. If we already have it in stock, we will tell you that too.
